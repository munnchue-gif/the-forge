# 10 · Pieces & LEGO (snap-on modularity)

Parent: [[00_FORGE_CONCEPT_MAP]]

## The philosophy — the war jeep
> "Who puts the water pump on top of the battery with the serpentine belt going
> to it? In war they build jeeps mechanically inclined — tools made out of the
> jeep, pull the door off and use it. I want things to WORK and snap on and off."

Design law derived from this: **every piece is a LEGO brick** — self-contained,
snaps on, snaps off, no hidden dependency spaghetti. Impracticality is the enemy.
A part you can't remove cleanly is a bug.

## The customize-first workflow (Eugene's stated method)
1. Identify each PIECE of the modularity puzzle (this whole map is that list).
2. **Customize each piece robustly BY ITSELF, ahead of time.**
3. THEN assemble the pre-perfected bricks → exceptional whole.
   (Not: dump it all out and glue after.)
