# 20 · The Wardrobe (recycle center)

Parent: [[00_FORGE_CONCEPT_MAP]]

## The idea
When a suit is stripped, you DON'T throw the parts away. The memories, vectors,
catch-files, and pieces go into a **wardrobe** — a recycle center. Because
something better today doesn't mean you won't want the old part again tomorrow.

This is the SAME principle as the map's own rule: peeled-off concepts go to
[[90_Wardrobe_Archive]], not the trash.

## The loop
1. A model/suit finishes or gets replaced.
2. [[21_The_Embedded_Tailor]] takes it apart, pulls ALL the data out.
3. Parts stored in the wardrobe (like clothes on a rack).
4. Those parts get **concocted** ([[Concoctinator]]) into a suit for the NEXT
   model we bring in.

## Already real
- WrapStore = the shelf; reclaim keeps wrap + vectors.
- Concoctinator strip() = the take-apart, keeps material.
GAP = the *tailor* that decides what to pull and how to rebuild → next page.
