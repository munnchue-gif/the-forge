# 00 · Forge — Concept Map (Living)

> This is NOT the whitepaper. This is the idea-tree — the place we lay out
> concepts, grow them, and keep the ones we peel off instead of deleting them.
> Drop this folder in Obsidian; the `[[links]]` render the whole map in graph view.
>
> Rule of this map (Eugene's): *nothing is thrown away.* When we find something
> better, the old piece goes to [[90_Wardrobe_Archive]] — the recycle center —
> because we may want it again. Just like stripping a suit and keeping the parts.

---

## The one idea everything hangs from

**[[01_The_Substance]]** — the alien fabric. The Forge (the original hard
skeleton) got *coated* in this substance, and everything it touched became
liquid, modular, sentient-ish. Capsules became **sandboxes**. Adapters became
**fabric that flows into whatever's needed**. Same environment, same world — but
"animated," 2D→3D, given a superpower while every other model in the same room
is still grinding. That leap is the thing we're chasing.

## Where Eugene feels we are

> "I think we're close. It doesn't get much better than when I took the gloves
> off and said *make the whole system out of the fabric.*"

Direction: **customize each PIECE robustly, ahead of time, THEN assemble** —
instead of laying it all out and gluing after. Each brick perfected on its own,
so the final snap-together is exceptional. When it reaches the point of being
"the alien thing that does everything — sentient, omnipresent" — *that's* when we
freeze it, offload to Ventoy, and start the next layer clean.
