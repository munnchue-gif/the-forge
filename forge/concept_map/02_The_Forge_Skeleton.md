It has no power of its own — power is *bonded on* (the NPU brain), never fused in.

Built + tested today (46/46 green):

- **SubstanceBus** — deaf sections + overseer's omnipresent tap.
- **Overseer** — Watcher (observe) + Commander (act-through-gate).
- **Wrap** — the mold that IS the training; the recycling-yard primitive.
- **VectorConduit** — the bond loop to the NPU brain; memory survives reclaim.
- **Concoctinator** — the sandbox/testing grounds.

This page = the skeleton. Everything else on the map is the substance *doing*
something on top of it.
