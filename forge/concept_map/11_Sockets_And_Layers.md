# 11 · Sockets & Layers (snap a whole capability on)

Parent: [[00_FORGE_CONCEPT_MAP]] · builds on [[10_Pieces_And_LEGO]]

## The idea
The Forge has sockets. Snap a layer onto a socket → the Forge *converts* into a
specialized pipeline for that job, carrying all the tools/suits/fabrics for it.

Example (Eugene's): **video creation.**
- Snap an "innate model / video layer" onto a socket.
- Forge becomes a content-creation pipeline: its own dossier/file system, its
  own concoctions, its own models + content for that path.
- It "grows up" into a content creator — becomes whatever the path needs.

Then: **legal work** needed instead? The video capsule **decouples**, gets
capsulized (see [[12_Capsule_Shrink_And_Restore]]), and a legal layer snaps on.

## The substance twist (why this isn't just plugins)
Because the Forge is wrapped in [[01_The_Substance]], a thing that attaches
doesn't just "plug in" — the fabric **creates an adapter for it automatically**
and overtakes/customizes it into the bare-minimum efficient form. Even non-
adapter tools get absorbed and reformed. Snapping on = getting baptized into the
fabric ([[03_The_Awakening]]).
