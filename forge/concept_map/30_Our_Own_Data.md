# 30 · Our Own Data (backwards on purpose)

Parent: [[00_FORGE_CONCEPT_MAP]] · fuels [[21_The_Embedded_Tailor]]

## The idea
> "We're doing things a little backwards — but really the world did it backwards
> and we're fixing it, making it manageable."

Plan:
- Don't train the tailor the normal way.
- **Scrape / observe how the world uses their data** — see what they did, see
  what it does.
- Run **ours** the same problems, **make our own calculations**, compare.
- Gather **our own data** shaped by how the world actually uses data — but
  structured our way (manageable, practical — the [[10_Pieces_And_LEGO]] ethic).

## Why
The bet: the gains come from *structure and practicality*, not from copying the
world's bloated, impractical approach (the water-pump-on-the-battery problem).
Learn from the world, then do it cleaner.
